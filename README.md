# Adidas Sales Analytics Dashboard Project

## Project Overview
This data analytics project focuses on analyzing Adidas sales data to explore sales performance, evaluate product profitability, and identify efficient sales channels and regional markets. An interactive dashboard was developed using Tableau to transform raw data into actionable visual insights that support strategic decision-making.

## Key Performance Indicators
Based on the analysis shown in the dashboard, the following overall performance figures were achieved:
* Total Sales: $899,902,125
* Total Profit: $332,134,761
* Total Units Sold: 2,478,861 units
* Average Price per Unit: $45.22
* Retailers Count: 6 major retailers
* Product Categories: 6 categories

## Data Insights and Recommendations

### Product Category Performance and Operating Margins
* Insight: The Men's Street Footwear category records the highest sales volume in terms of units sold with 593,320 units. Conversely, women's products including apparel and athletic footwear yield the highest operating margins, ranging between 44.13% and 44.61%.
* Strategic Recommendation: Marketing investments should be scaled up for women's products due to their superior profit margins. Concurrently, a robust inventory flow must be maintained for Men's Street Footwear to consistently meet its massive consumer demand and stabilize cash flow.

### Sales Channels and Consumer Preferences
* Insight: Traditional In-store sales lead the channels with a total of $356.64M, followed closely by Outlet sales at $295.59M, and Online sales at $247.67M.
* Strategic Recommendation: While direct physical retail remains dominant, digital channels represent a high-growth opportunity with lower operational overhead. It is recommended to optimize the e-commerce user experience and launch online-exclusive promotions to accelerate digital market share.

### Geographic Distribution and Regional Profitability
* Insight: The West region stands out as the most profitable area with an operating profit of $89.61M, followed by the Northeast. At the state level, New York ($64.23M), California ($60.17M), and Florida ($59.28M) are the top three contributors to total sales.
* Strategic Recommendation: Focus future retail expansions and regional marketing budgets heavily on the West and Northeast regions. Additionally, deepen local distributor networks within the high-performing hub states to solidify market dominance.

### Retailers Performance Analysis
* Insight: West Gear drives the highest sales volume at $242.96M, followed by Foot Locker at $220.09M, while Walmart ranks at the bottom of the list with $74.56M.
* Strategic Recommendation: Strengthen strategic partnerships with West Gear and Foot Locker by providing volume incentives, exclusive releases, or preferred credit terms, as they serve as the primary engine for Adidas retail distribution.

### Sales Trend and Time-Series Analysis
* Insight: The sales timeline illustrates a strong upward trajectory in total revenue starting from early 2021 through 2022, accompanied by distinct seasonal fluctuations and sharp demand spikes during specific times of the year.
* Strategic Recommendation: Implement predictive analytics for proactive inventory management well ahead of peak seasons. This timing strategy prevents stockouts on high-demand items during recurring seasonal spikes.

## Tools and Technologies Used
* Tableau Public and Desktop: For data cleaning, feature visualization, and interactive dashboard architecture design.
* Dataset: Official Adidas Sales Dataset.

## How to Run and Interact with the Dashboard
1. Download the packaged workbook file (.twbx) or visit the project link hosted on Tableau Public.
2. Open the file using Tableau Desktop or Tableau Reader.
3. You can fully interact with the dashboard by clicking on specific regions, retailers, or sales methods to dynamically update all KPIs and visualizations automatically.
